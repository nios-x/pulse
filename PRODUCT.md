# Product

## Pitch

One place for a family to manage everyone's health: records, medicines, appointments and emergency info, with role-based access for each member.

## Users

- **The organiser**: a tech-savvy adult (30–45) who runs the family's health, often for parents in another city.
- **Elderly parents**: low tech confidence, reading glasses, prefer Hindi or another regional language. Need big targets, plain words, voice.
- **Caregivers**: a spouse, sibling or paid helper who needs limited access to specific people.

## Principles

- **One calm place.** Every screen answers "how is everyone doing, and what do I do now?"
- **The right access for each person.** Roles are visible, enforced on the server, and explained when something is disabled.
- **Danger is never subtle, and never colour-only.** Status always has an icon and a word.
- **Responsible AI.** Safety rules first, a triage level never a diagnosis, AI can only make advice more cautious.
- **Private by default.** Consent, encryption, time-limited sharing, an activity log.

## Hackathon checklist

- Sign-up with family profiles (self, parent, child) ✔
- Medicine reminders with a taken/missed log ✔ (email + in-app, cron)
- Health records storage (reports, prescriptions) ✔ (encrypted)
- Doctor and teleconsult booking ✔
- Privacy basics: consent, encrypted data, time-limited sharing ✔
- AI symptom triage with a safety layer ✔
- Prescription scanner ✔
- Voice-first, regional languages ✔ (speech input + read aloud, 10 languages)
- Drug interaction checker + generic alternatives ✔
